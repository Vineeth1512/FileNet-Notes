#  What is IBM Filenet
Filenet is the ECM  platform where we can store the documents and  runs the workflowss autmatically.  if we use filenet it reduces the manual work , speeds up approvals , it ensures documents stored securly and traceble.

---
# What is PE 
PE is the workflow engine of the filenet it responsible for running managing , monitoring the workflows it does not store documents it moves the workflows form one step to another step.

# What is Work flow 
Workflow is an automatic movement of work from one step to another
